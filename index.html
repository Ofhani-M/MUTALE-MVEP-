Safety First

Immediate response

Your safety comes before everything else. We listen to what is happening, help you think through safe options and connect you with the police, shelters and emergency services when needed. You stay in control of every decision.

Safety Planning Emergency Contacts Referrals Speak to Someone
🤝

Victim Support

Ongoing, at your pace

We walk alongside survivors of crime, abuse and gender-based violence. This includes emotional support, help understanding the reporting process and accompaniment to access services such as clinics, courts and social workers.

Emotional Support Reporting Help Accompaniment Access to Services
💛

Counselling & Healing

Referral and follow-up

Trauma leaves marks that deserve care. We help you reach counselling and other essential services, so you can process what happened, rebuild trust and begin to heal.

Counselling Access Trauma Support Safe Listening Follow-up

Education & Empowerment

Community programmes

Prevention starts with knowledge. We run community awareness and empowerment programmes that teach people their rights, how to recognise abuse and how to support someone who is being harmed.
